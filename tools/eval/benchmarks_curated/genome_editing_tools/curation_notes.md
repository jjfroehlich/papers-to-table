## Papers considered but rejected and why
Anzalone 2019 prime editing, Gaudelli 2017 ABE, Hu 2018 xCas9, Koblan 2018 BEmax, and Walton 2020 SpRY were considered. They were rejected for this artifact because the available PMC PDF endpoint saved placeholder HTML rather than a real PDF under the current tooling.

## Final inclusion rationale
The final papers form a compact editor-engineering set with recurring columns for modality, editor name, selected variant, efficiency, and architecture.

## Notes about PDF quality
All final PDFs begin with %PDF and include main figures. Nature Communications PDFs are text PDFs with embedded figures.

## Notes about figures or schematics
Inspect Fig. 1 panels first for editor/module schematics, then result figures with variant comparisons.

## Any uncertainty or manual follow-up needed
Human annotation should verify whether highest efficiency should be global maximum or maximum in the authors primary representative assay before locking gold values.
