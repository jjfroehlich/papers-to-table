# Curation Notes

## Exact search queries used
paper-search-mcp PubMed broad query: molecular neuroscience synaptic mechanism perturbation imaging electrophysiology assay 2018 2024. Web queries: site:elifesciences.org/articles molecular neuroscience synaptic perturbation imaging electrophysiology 2019 eLife PDF; site:elifesciences.org/articles synaptic plasticity molecular mechanism perturbation electrophysiology 2020 eLife neuroscience PDF.

## Papers considered but rejected and why
Computational-only synaptic theory papers and broad method-only imaging hardware papers were considered but rejected because they lacked a clear perturbation plus molecular mechanism target. A 2025 eLife synapse-elimination paper was rejected to keep the set mostly 2018-2021.

## Final inclusion rationale
The final papers balance molecular mechanism, perturbation, imaging, and electrophysiology while keeping common extraction columns.

## Notes about PDF quality
All final eLife PDFs begin with %PDF and include figures and legends in the main PDF.

## Notes about figures or schematics
Inspect reporter/schematic figures for MN02 and primary electrophysiology or imaging panels for MN01, MN03, MN04, and MN05.

## Any uncertainty or manual follow-up needed
Gold annotators should decide whether to use the authors headline quantitative result or a specific representative figure panel when several assays support the same conclusion.
