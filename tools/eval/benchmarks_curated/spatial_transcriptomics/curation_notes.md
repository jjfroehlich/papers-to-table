## Papers considered but rejected and why
Stahl 2016 Spatial Transcriptomics, Rodriques 2019 Slide-seq, Stickels 2021 Slide-seqV2, Maynard 2021 spatialLIBD, and Liu 2020 DBiT-seq were considered. Several were rejected here because PMC PDF download saved placeholder HTML or because the final source was not directly downloadable through the current tooling.

## Final inclusion rationale
The final papers keep shared columns around platform, tissue, resolution/capture unit, output, and representative spatial figure panels.

## Notes about PDF quality
All final Nature Communications PDFs begin with %PDF and include spatial maps or platform schematics.

## Notes about figures or schematics
Inspect Fig. 1 schematics for platform/capture-unit details and later figures for tissue maps, spatial clusters, or cell-type annotations.

## Any uncertainty or manual follow-up needed
Manual annotation should record the exact spatial resolution wording from the paper, because platform papers may report pixel size, spot/capture unit, or single-cell status differently.
